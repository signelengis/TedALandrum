# Aircraft image manifest — Ted A. Landrum site

17 images. Number 00 is the 1943 Pensacola portrait of Ted, hosted by Find a Grave — **download this one and self-host it**, since Find a Grave may block hotlinking. The rest are from Wikimedia Commons. Download each URL, save it under the
**Save as** filename, and commit them to your repo (suggested folder: `images/`).

Then open the site and set the **imageBase** tweak to your raw GitHub URL, e.g.
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YOURNAME/YOURREPO/main/images`
and every image switches over to your copy.
