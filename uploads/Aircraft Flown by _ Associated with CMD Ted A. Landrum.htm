Boeing-Stearman N2S-3 Kaydet
WWII · Primary Trainer

Boeing-Stearman N2S-3 Kaydet

Classic open-cockpit biplane used to train nearly every WWII Navy pilot.

Landrum was flying Bureau Number 3500 when it crashed near Robertsdale, Alabama on Sept. 3, 1943, during his Pensacola flight training. He survived and went on to earn his wings.

Douglas AD-4N Skyraider
Korean War · Attack / Night-Attack

Douglas A-1 Skyraider (AD-4N)

Rugged single-engine attack aircraft; the AD-4N was the night-attack variant.

Flown with VC-11 aboard USS Philippine Sea during the Korean War.

Korean War · Airborne Early Warning

Douglas A-1 Skyraider (AD-4W)

Radar early-warning variant of the Skyraider, easily identified by the large belly-mounted radome.

This photo is specifically of a VC-11 AD-4W in flight off Korea circa 1952 — Landrum's own squadron.

Beechcraft Twin Beech 18
Civilian · Corporate Transport

Beechcraft Twin Beech 18

Twin-engine light transport; Landrum flew both the classic taildragger version and the tricycle-gear "Super 18."
